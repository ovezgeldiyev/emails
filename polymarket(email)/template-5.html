Everyone was wrong

Background

When the papal conclave began, the market was betting heavily on two favorites:

Cardinal Parolin at 30 cents (30% chance)

Cardinal Tagle at 22 cents (22% chance)

Homework

While other traders bet on name recognition, Domer dug deeper:

The big bet

Domer didn't just make one bet, he:

The moment of truth

"A few minutes later, my jaw dropped as Prevost -- the guy I had just amassed shares in at 200-1 like 20 minutes earlier -- walked out onto the balcony as Pope."
